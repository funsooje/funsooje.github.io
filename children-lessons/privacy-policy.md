# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 2026_

This application, **Children's Lessons**, is provided as a free digital Sunday School lesson book for The Apostolic Faith Church's children's lessons, including lesson text, songs, and illustrations.

We respect your privacy and want to be transparent about how the app works.

---

## Information We Collect

- **No personal data is collected.**  
  The app does not ask for or store any personal information (such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers). There are no user accounts.

- **No tracking or analytics.**  
  We do not use analytics tools, advertising networks, or any third-party trackers.

---

## How the App Works

- The app downloads lesson content (text, songs, images, and sheet music) from our own content server when first opened, and stores it locally on the device for offline access.
- Updates to lesson content are only checked and downloaded when the user explicitly requests them from the app's Settings screen.
- **No data is ever uploaded from your device.**

---

## Sharing of Information

Since no information is collected, no data is shared with third parties.

---

## Children's Privacy

This app is designed for children's Sunday School use (**rated Everyone**). It contains no ads, in-app purchases, or external links.

---

## Changes to This Policy

If future versions of the app introduce new features that affect privacy, this policy will be updated here.
